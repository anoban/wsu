species level mycorrhizal state updates for the FRED 4.0 subset

iteration 01
------------

- extracted mycorrhizal states from FRED 4.0 (the whole dataset) (had multiple entries per species, for many species).
- extracted mycorrhizal states from Maherali et. al. 2016.
- extracted mycorrhizal states from FungalRoot species level dataset (had multiple entries per species, for many species).
- wherever a species had multiple and conflicting state info in FRED 4.0 and FungalRoot species level dataset, it was resolved 
  manually by combining states where it was intuitive (AM & EcM into EcMAM, AM & NM into NMAM).
- where this combination was less intuitive (the resultant state is not very common), FungalRoot genus level state recommendations were used.
- once the hand cleanup was over, merged all together to create a single dataset
- had 264 state missing species

iteration 02
--------------

- the whole Acer genus was made AM (Luke's advice)
- all species of Quercus were made EcM (Luke's advice)
- updated all state missing species in Lamiaceae to AM (Brundrett & Tedersoo 2018)
